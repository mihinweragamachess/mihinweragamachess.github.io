AIM Mihin Mindula Weragama

Who is Mihin?

Mihin Mindula Weragama, born in 2009, is a Sri Lankan chess player dedicated to mastering the game, with a particular focus on Bullet chess. He holds FIDE International Ratings across all three over-the-board time controls, Classical, Rapid, and Blitz, and is also a FIDE-titled player, holding the Arena International Master (AIM) title. His chess profile reflects extensive experience in Bullet and fast-paced chess, supported by exceptional tactical skills, speed, and practical playing ability.

Bullet Career

He began his journey in Bullet chess in 2023, quickly demonstrating a natural talent for the fast-paced time control. In 2024, he achieved his first victory against a titled player by defeating FM Sunil Weeramantry, marking an important milestone in his Bullet career. Since then, he has gone on to defeat numerous chess masters while developing a strong command of Bullet and fast chess. Much of his Bullet experience and training has taken place on Lichess, where he has played more than 7,000 Bullet games, reaching a peak rating of 2436. He has also reached a peak Bullet rating of 2219 on Chess.com, despite playing there less frequently.

Mihin's official FIDE Arena journey began in January 2025, when he registered on the platform. On January 22, 2025, he earned the Arena Candidate Master (ACM) title, followed just five days later by the Arena FIDE Master (AFM) title on January 27, 2025, after reaching a rating of 1672. At that stage, he was already aiming for the Arena International Master (AIM) title, which required a 1700 rating, but continued training before making another serious push. After extensive Bullet training, Mihin returned to FIDE Arena in July 2026, where he produced an outstanding performance with 723 wins, 116 losses, and 22 draws, reaching a peak Bullet rating of 2023. During this remarkable run, he became the #1 active FIDE Arena Bullet player in Sri Lanka and entered the Top 150 worldwide. He also achieved the AIM (Arena International Master) title very easily in July 22, 2026.
